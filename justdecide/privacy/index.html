我来定 隐私政策

JustDecide Privacy Policy

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 25 日

Effective date: August 25, 2026

我来定（JustDecide）是一款纯本地的决策工具。我们非常重视你的隐私。

1. 数据收集：本应用不收集、不存储、不上传任何个人数据。你的决策记录、选项、模板等内容 均只保存在你的设备本地，我们无法访问，也无法看到。

2. 网络：本应用无任何网络请求，不连接任何服务器，不使用任何第三方统计或广告 SDK。

3. 内容安全：为保护用户，本应用在本地对输入内容做敏感词提醒，该过程完全在你的设备上进行， 不会发送或保存相关内容。

4. 第三方共享：本应用不与任何第三方共享数据。

5. 应用内购买：应用内购买由 Apple 处理。我们无法获取你的完整支付信息。

本隐私政策可能不定期更新，更新后将在本页面公布。

JustDecide (我来定) is a fully on-device decision tool. Your privacy matters to us.

1. Data collection: this app does not collect, store, or upload any personal data. Your decisions, options, and templates stay on your device, and we cannot access or see them.

2. Network: this app makes no network requests, connects to no servers, and uses no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

3. Content safety: to protect users, the app checks input for sensitive words locally on your device. Nothing is sent or stored in this process.

4. Third-party sharing: this app does not share data with any third party.

5. In-app purchases: in-app purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your full payment information.

This policy may be updated from time to time; updates will be posted on this page.
