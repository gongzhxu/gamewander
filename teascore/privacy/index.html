TeaScore Privacy Policy

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 17 日

Effective date: August 17, 2026

TeaScore 当前将测试题目、测试结果、语言和购买解锁状态保存在你的设备本地，以提供测试、 历史记录和恢复购买等功能。我们不会在当前版本中创建账号，也不会将这些内容上传至我们的服务器。

应用内购买由 Apple 处理。我们无法获取你的完整支付信息。

如未来版本改变数据处理方式，我们会在更新应用前同步更新本政策。

TeaScore stores your quiz questions, results, language, and purchase-unlock state locally on your device to provide testing, history, and purchase restoration. We do not create accounts in the current version, nor upload this content to our servers.

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your full payment information.

If a future version changes how data is handled, we will update this policy before releasing the update.
